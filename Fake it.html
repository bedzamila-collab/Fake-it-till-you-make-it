SPEAKING ACTIVITIES 🗣️
Complete the following 5 interactive speaking tasks:

Activity 1: Press Office Interview (Role-play)

You are a real Duke PA student. An investigative reporter interviews you about Angel's fake story. Answer the questions using target vocabulary:

• "When did you first notice something was off?"
• "How realistic was her content about the grueling routine?"
• "What advice would you give followers who get scammed by influencers?"

Activity 2: Debate — "Fake It Till You Make It"

Is pretending to be qualified ever acceptable in modern social media culture?

Speaker A: Argue that self-promotion requires exaggeration and fake confidence until real skills follow.
Speaker B: Argue that faking qualifications in medical/legal/educational spheres is dangerous fraud.

Activity 3: Ethical Dilemma Cards

Pick one scenario and explain how you would react:

Card A: You discover your colleague lied on their CV about having a Master's degree, but they do a great job. Do you expose them?
Card B: A friend is buying fake followers to get brand deals. They ask for your help filming content. What do you say?

Activity 4: Two Truths and a Lie (Influencer Edition)

Tell your partner 3 stories about something impressive you or someone you know did (2 true, 1 completely made up using target phrases like "you can't make this stuff up" or "took it a step further"). Your partner must guess the fake story!

Activity 5: Crisis PR Strategy Meeting

Imagine you are the PR team for a cosmetic brand (e.g. Sephora) that sponsored Angel. Draft a 1-minute public statement explaining how you got scammed and what steps you are taking to protect consumers.
